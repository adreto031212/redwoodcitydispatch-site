Redwood City's three self-service sandbag stations are open around the clock

November 19, 2026 · Redwood City Dispatch, issue 1 · All Redwood City, San Carlos and Belmont news

They are at the Public Works Services Yard on Broadway, at the end of Radio Road near the dog park, and in the Fair Oaks Community Center parking lot; the limit is 24 bags per resident. The city's September storm report put the chance of a very strong El Niño this winter above 90%. Blocked storm drain, fallen tree or flooding: Public Works, (650) 780-7464. City of Redwood City
