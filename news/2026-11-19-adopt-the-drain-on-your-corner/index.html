adopt the drain on your corner

November 19, 2026 · Redwood City Dispatch, issue 1 · All Redwood City, San Carlos and Belmont news

Leaves on a storm drain grate are how a rainy night becomes a flooded intersection. Redwood City's new Adopt-a-Drain program asks you to pick a drain near you, give it a name, and clear the leaves and trash off the top of the grate twice a month through the rainy season; log each visit online and report anything under the grate to Public Works.
