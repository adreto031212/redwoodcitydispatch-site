Heads up

  • No school for Redwood City School District TK-8 students, Wednesday to Friday, November 25 to 27. Wednesday is a no-school day and Thursday and Friday are the holiday break; plan care for all three days. Redwood City School District
  • North Fair Oaks Community Council, tonight, Thursday, November 19, 7 to 9 PM, Fair Oaks Community Center on Middlefield Road, or on Zoom. What the city decided and what changed

San Carlos is weighing a ban on gas-powered leaf blowers

The council held a study session on November 9.

Redwood City named Sarah La Torra its library director on November 10

She has led the Redwood City Public Library as interim director since 2024 and first joined in 2007 as a teen services librarian; she helped build the library's Makerspace.

Redwood City's three self-service sandbag stations are open around the clock

They are at the Public Works Services Yard on Broadway, at the end of Radio Road near the dog park, and in the Fair Oaks Community Center parking lot; the limit is 24 bags per resident.
